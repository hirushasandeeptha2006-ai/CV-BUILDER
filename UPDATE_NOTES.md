# CV Generator Updates

## Changes Made

### 1. **CV Image Upload Feature** ✅
The image upload feature **already exists** in your CV generator! You can find it in the **Personal tab**.

**How to use it:**
1. Click on the **Personal** tab in the sidebar
2. You'll see a circular photo placeholder at the top
3. Click the camera icon (bottom-right of the circle) to upload your photo
4. The photo will appear in all templates that support profile pictures

### 2. **Modern Elegant Template - Color Bar Enhancement** ✅
Added a stylish **vertical color bar accent** on the left side of the Modern Elegant template.

**What was added:**
- A 6mm wide gradient color bar running from top to bottom
- Teal gradient colors (from #5f9ea0 via #4a8588 to #5f9ea0)
- Added shadow effect for depth
- The color bar appears in both the template preview thumbnails and the actual CV

**Visual Impact:**
- Creates a modern, premium look
- Adds visual hierarchy and draws the eye
- Separates content elegantly
- Matches modern CV design trends

## Templates Overview

Your CV generator now has **4 professional templates**:

1. **Classic Pro** - Professional header with right sidebar
2. **Modern Elegant** - Teal-themed with NEW vertical color bar accent ⭐
3. **Business Circular** - Blue sidebar with circular design elements  
4. **Bold Modern** - Dark sidebar with bold typography

## How to Test

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Go to the **Design** tab to select templates
3. Select **Modern Elegant** to see the new color bar
4. Go to the **Personal** tab to upload a photo
5. Watch your photo appear in the CV preview!
